**Verdict: approve the design; two blockers to close in the implementation scope, neither changes the plan's ownership model.** The plan correctly keeps one owner (the journal), one committed selector (root), and applies bytes at `stage` where all three lanes already converge. The genuine encoding bounds survive: the prepared genesis block stays under the 256 KiB block ceiling in `quod_ledger.erl:136` and the initial diff under 192 KiB in `quod_ontology.erl:883`, so even `unlimited` is capped at roughly 450 KiB plus config.

## Blockers

1. **Byte-pressure reason cannot reach the caller in two of three lanes as the tree stands.** Staging for the atomic and independent lanes happens at attestation in `quod_proof_session.erl:638` (reserve, stage, bind_reservation), not in the frozen tree. When the target scope is remote or co-hosted, the stage error crosses as a scope error and `quod_scope_wire.erl:918` is a closed vocabulary. Anything outside it is rewritten to `{protocol_error, proof_engine}` by `normalize_public_error` at line 984, and `public_scope_reason` in `quod_prolog.erl:3356` routes through the same normaliser. The plan must add one bounded entry to that catalog, for example `{effect_custody_limit, prepared_bytes, Actual, Limit}` with uint64 validation, and `encode_staged` at `quod_effect_journal.erl:557` must stop folding the size result into `invalid_direct_effect`. Apply the byte check after the digest check so an invalid preparation is never reported as over budget.

2. **Count pressure must keep its retryable class.** Today `reserve` returns `busy`, which the single lane maps to `{rejected, retry}` at `quod_prolog.erl:6472` and the scope path to `{ontology_busy, Ns}` at line 3383. The plan's "count/byte pressure must identify the resource" would, if applied literally to count, replace a transient retryable class with an unknown term that the wire rejects as `bad_error_code`. Byte pressure is permanent for that request and needs a new non-retryable class. Count pressure stays `busy`; report its detail through `stats/0` and metrics only. State this split in the plan.

## Nonblockers

- **Frozen tree is incomplete for the claimed scope.** It omits `quod_proof_session.erl`, `quod_scope_session.erl` (bind_group at 957, bind_operation at 976), `quod_scope_wire.erl` and `quod_ingress_limits.hrl`. The implementation review tree must include them or "every production caller" cannot be checked.
- **Where the live refusal actually happened.** It was proof-time, in `stage_prepared_action` at `quod_ontology_predicates.erl:173` via `prepared_bytes`, surfacing as `{system_error, invalid_prepared_effect}`. After the change the refusal moves to stage time, after sealing but before `checkpoint_bound_effect`, so still before binding or commit. A too-large preparation now costs a full proof first. Acceptable and minimal.
- **Race semantics verified against code.** Reserve counts active plus reservations at `quod_effect_journal.erl:300`; bind paths consume `staged` without re-encoding, and `make_room_for_active_row` at line 1689 never refuses a reserved slot. Tightening after stage therefore cannot cancel, as the plan says. Keep the new install function count-only for trimming, as `trim_terminal_rows` at line 1673 is today.
- **Committed versus staged policy verified.** The selector runs read-only on the committed program with no caller overlay at `quod_runtime.erl:2855`. The setter is not a public goal. Update `root_public_goal` at `quod_root.pl:50` and the comment block at lines 76 to 80 for the new arity, and demand exactly one `{Count, Bytes}` solution in the selector at `quod_runtime.erl:2772`.
- **Startup and unchanged I/O already hold.** `install_capacity` at line 1665 is a no-op on equal values; journal owner refresh re-queues selection at `quod_runtime.erl:874`. The snapshot needs payload version 8; the existing clause at line 1476 already rejects every other version explicitly.
- **Decode guard.** Removing the size guard from `decode_prepared` at `quod_ontology.erl:652` is fine because the bytes are digest-bound at load and at stage. Keep the `is_binary` guard.
- **Callers to update beyond the plan's list.** Metrics at `quod_metrics.erl:743`, the capacity-only trace in `quod_root_resource_lifecycle_tests.erl:163`, the gen_call observer at `quod_lifecycle_single_path_tests.erl:2426`, and the public-goal assertions at lines 77 to 98 of that file.
- **Deployment.** A surviving version 7 `direct_effects.qej` makes journal `init` crash by design. The candidate refound must wipe the journal file with the ledgers.
