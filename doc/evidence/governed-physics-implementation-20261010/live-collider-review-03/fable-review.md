**Recovery and parking.** Reader death with a published height rebuilds once; a replacement that dies before publishing parks as `unreachable` until follow progress restarts it (`quod_foreign_log.erl:4026-4046`, `:3774`). This is bounded and cannot spin. Coalescing always surfaces the newest state, and a rebuilt materializer publishes with `From = 0`, so the runtime sees a resnapshot and reselects (`:3966-3977`, `quod_runtime.erl:1852-1860`).
