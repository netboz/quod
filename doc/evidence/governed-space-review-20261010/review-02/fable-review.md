Status: I've finished reading the governing docs, the draft ontology, the tests, and the ingress/fixture/overlay seams the draft depends on. Writing the review now.

**Verdict.** The draft's state model (owner slot with retained revisions plus destination placement, atomic two-writer admission, fail-closed cleanup hook) is sound and matches AGENTS.md and world.md. It is not acceptable yet: the tests cannot demonstrate cross-owner authorization because one principal signs everything, the library violates the reasoned-failure contract, and the internal slot writer is publicly callable. The cleanup seam is adequate as a prerequisite shape but the plan text over-claims what it delivers.

## Findings

**Demonstrated defects**

- **D1 (high) One principal signs every request, so no ACL claim is tested.** `sign/2` uses the node actor (test/quod_space_tests.erl:146-149, Node at line 107). Both destinations grant that principal everything (line 130) plus an anchored-goal catch-all (lines 141-142), and the owner fixture grants it everything too (test/quod_agent_hosting_tests.erl:1464). "Revocation after the subject loses its grant" (lines 52-56) is the same principal granting itself. Participant-call rejection (lines 33, 37) is exercised only by absent staged state, never by policy.
- **D2 (high) No reasoned failures.** doc/proofs.md:60-73 requires `fail_with_reason/1` for revision mismatch, held exclusive binding, and missing or ambiguous configuration. space.pl fails bare everywhere (lines 57-60, 68, 84, 108, 121-125). Consequently `denied/2` (lines 157-160) asserts only `{failed, _}` and several denials pass for undetermined reasons, for example line 28 fails on class or on consent. Ambiguous assignment rows (line 60) are indistinguishable from a stale revision; compare quod_physics.pl:77-81.
- **D3 (medium) `space_assign/5` is a public predicate** (space.pl:112-117). Under any `can_invoke(_, P, _, _)` catch-all, which the fixtures and lobby_instance.pl:4 both install, a principal sets any assignment directly, bypassing consent and the destination. The plan says it must not be in a public ACL but ships no mechanism.
- **D4 (medium) Tests call the wrong APIs.** Line 66 expects a normalized tuple; resolve_operation returns `{ok, _, {operation_outcome, _, #{status := committed}}}` (src/quod_client_goal_ingress.erl:256-262; used correctly in test/quod_physics_tests.erl:1034). `quod_reg:register/1` and `unregister/1` (lines 74, 102) do not exist; the module exports `reg/1` (src/quod_reg.erl:47) and the precedent unregisters with `gproc:unreg(quod_reg:name(...))` (hosting tests line 126). The loser shape at line 93 is asserted for a two-writer atomic abort, but the only precedent is single-writer `conflict_retry` (hosting tests lines 548-550). Verify the atomic-abort normalization and assert the reason.
- **D5 (low) Test 2 leaks blocked proof workers.** The `after` block (lines 99-102) kills submitters only; workers parked in `receive release` (hosting tests lines 28-31) survive until proof timeout and collide with the fixture teardown at lines 122-124.
- **D6 (low) `no_simulation/1` is vacuous.** No ontology in the fixture composes quod_physics.pl, so `physics_projection/2` is undefined and the selector is absent by construction (src/quod_runtime.erl:2534-2538). It cannot show that space declarations leave physics empty where physics vocabulary exists.
- **D7 (low) History preservation is not checked.** Lines 61-63 read an attribute nothing touched. Assert genesis anchors and applied heights across restart instead.

**Potential concerns**

- **P1 (high) Cleanup seam versus acceptance.** `space_physical_change/3` is only a hook shape. The physics vocabulary has no committed body-to-entity binding: `physics_body/7` is keyed by numeric id and the only entity link is the view mark `depicts/3` (quod_physics.pl:236-240). A correct `withdrawn` clause for a simulated space cannot be authored in this slice. The seam is adequate as a prerequisite: it runs inside the destination transaction and rollback is demonstrated (test lines 40-43). Plan item 5 must stop claiming body/constraint/checkpoint cleanup is performed and name the binding fact as the first deliverable of the physics task.
- **P2 (medium) No unilateral exit.** Withdrawal and eviction both need a live atomic group across both ontologies (space.pl:91-110). A permanently absent destination leaves the owner slot held forever; an absent source leaves the destination unable to evict. Fail-closed is correct under world.md:244-247, but doc/spaces.md must say so and name the explicit policy exit as future work.
- **P3 (medium) Re-entrant staged view is an engine property the bundle cannot verify.** The callbacks at space.pl:83-84 and 107-108 work only because the origin scope is reused (src/quod_prolog.erl:4380-4388; doc/proofs.md:21-24). The modules implementing nested `::` back to the origin are not in the frozen tree, and the only evidence is the parent's run log. The OCC consequence is right: the re-entrant read captures the owner's `physical_assignment` token first-read-wins (src/quod_erlog_db_local_prove.erl:1001-1013), which is what makes competing admissions conflict.
- **P4 (medium) The ACL table is unwritten and the fixture hides it.** The principal crosses `::` unchanged (fipa_request.pl:12). So for organizational admission the caller is the destination owner, and the source's `can_enter_space` fact and `can_invoke` must name that principal for anchored `space_consent/4` and `space_withdrawal_consent/4`. For placement the caller is at the source, so the destination must admit the instance owner's principal for anchored `space_accept_placement/3` and `space_release_placement/4`, and for eviction the source must admit the destination's principal for `space_withdraw/3` plus the re-entrant anchored `space_assignment/3` read.
- **P5 (low) Self-containment and placement cycles are not refused.** `space_contain(catalogue, ref(A, catalogue))` on A itself passes under policy; two physical spaces can each hold the other because exclusive slots are per entity. Non-transitive `space_contains` means no engine loop. Refuse self structurally; leave longer cycles to policy and say so.
- **Confirmed adequate:** owner slot `(Revision, Destination)` plus destination `(Space, Subject, Revision)` is sufficient and minimal. The destination in the slot is what lets only the named space consume a staged slot or release it; the placement revision is what fences the retract; retained empty-slot revisions cover ABA (test lines 50-51). Transfer is refused at space.pl:68. Classes cannot be placed physically (line 66). Nothing in space.pl reacts, hosts, or selects resources.
