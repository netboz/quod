### Specific remaining D6 ruling

The unresolved decision is **what a selectable ontology-space contents subject
is: an entire `Name/Arity` predicate, an individual ordered clause, or both**,
and which identity survives clause reordering, replacement, duplication or
predicate rename/deletion. Its authored layout and exterior/contents recipe
must refer to that exact subject, separately from scene occurrence paths.
A positional clause index, text digest or automatically allocated entity ID
would each invent different continuity semantics; none has been selected.

This choice is constrained by [spaces](../../doc/spaces.md): source ownership
is separate from spatial assignment, and depiction does not confer space
semantics. Current [reflection](../../priv/ontologies/common_predicates.pl)
still declares `isa(ontology,thing)` and exact `ontology_ref(Ns,Anchor)`.
The [source editor](../../priv/ontologies/gui_tools.pl) exposes a predicate
indicator catalogue and guarded whole/predicate source; across-predicate order
is presentation, **within-predicate clause order is semantic**. Existing guarded
edits preserve unchanged prefixes and recheck exact source, rather than exposing
stable clause entities. D6 must preserve these proof/cut/order and concurrent
edit guarantees, same-agent/exclusive assignment, exact source authorization,
and separate founding/recovery lifecycle under the
[multiwrite contract](../../doc/multiwrite-architecture.md). Class and Eidolon
recipes may share their ontology. None of this requires a new mutable predicate
registry or authorizes inventing the missing identity rule.
