The inherited `build-applied.py` and this campaign’s `runtime02` omitted `erlog/rebar.config.script`. They therefore compiled the legacy `dict` binding backend, whereas the actual dependency configuration selects `HAS_MAPS` on this OTP. **All timings above are current-source/default-compiler diagnostics, not configured-runtime performance.** This also qualifies the previous applied-build/production compile reports: matching source/header is insufficient evidence of matching dependency compiler configuration. Existing semantic test results remain literal for their tested artifacts.
