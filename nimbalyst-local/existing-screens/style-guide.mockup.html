World surface and glass panel

World: radial highlight top-right over a 145° teal gradient, with a perspective grid below the horizon. Panels float as translucent glass (only the tint is translucent; text and controls stay opaque). Shadow 0 16px 48px #0005. Left rail 240–280px, main panel fluid, gap 24px, max width 1080–1180px.

Controls and feedback

Minimum control height 48px (56px in larger-text mode), radius 10px, 1px gray edge, raised surface #304C56, hover #416471. Primary = gold fill with teal-dark text. Disabled = dashed edge, dimmed. Focus ring: 3px gold, 4px offset. Range and checkbox accent gold. Status strip at the panel foot: neutral muted, error #FFB8BD, done #C5E7A0. Pending list items are full-width buttons; the active one gets a 2px gold border. Nothing steals focus or opens a modal.

Vocabulary used in UI text

Plain words. "Representation" (not eidolon) when facing the user; "lens" for what is looked at and why; "mark" only in technical detail. Reads disclose knowledge; invocation and edits are authorized separately by the current owner. Outcomes are stated: Draft, Submitted, Applied, Outcome unknown, Refused with the reason.
