# Quod GUI, space and VR specification conformance review

The experience demonstrated to Yan does not satisfy the requested interaction model. Important domain mechanisms work, but an instance's ontology-authored GUI is presented through a global inspector rather than as a representation associated with that instance's visible Eidolon. This is a missing original requirement, not an optional enhancement. Software slice acceptance and reproducible packages do not establish product acceptance.

This is the coordinator's independent review, dated 2026-11-01. Claude Fable's separately requested read-only review is complete and reconciled below. The sole implementation writer completed its independent 45-item inventory and stopped further GUI restructuring pending a bounded correction instruction. No hardware opportunity is live.

## Why the delivery missed the goal

The verification concentrated on whether individual native controls were reachable, signed actions were correct, drafts survived and artifacts were reproducible. Those are necessary safeguards. They did not test the decisive product question: does selecting this visible instance expose its ontology-authored GUI in an understandable relationship to that occurrence?

The missing binding was explicitly written into the original design. The coordinator incorrectly carried it as an open/deferred item while treating successive global-panel improvements as progress toward the requested experience. The initial273 response also misidentified the user's objection as primarily attention hierarchy and shading. Steering274 corrected that interpretation. This is a planning and acceptance failure as well as an adapter gap.

The delivered backend should not be discarded: it already supplies exact-subject offers, owned forms, guarded actions, current navigation, original-outcome recovery and retained drafts. The correction must connect those existing capabilities to the intended representation model, without building another domain or GUI state owner.

## Correction order and acceptance

1. Complete the source-backed review and reconcile the writer's and Claude's findings. Mark disputed or unverified claims explicitly; review feedback is not automatic architecture authority.
2. Trace and complete the existing recipe-to-form-to-occurrence placement contract. Carry the selected occurrence context alongside the exact semantic subject. Preserve the same native form/controller when presenting near that occurrence or in the personal workspace. Use the existing scene/GUI owners and preserve independent occurrence lifetimes and authorization withdrawal.
3. Qualify that vertical interaction with a space and an independent non-space instance: recognizable selected subject; authored information/actions; ordinary selection places the form near its visual representation; deliberate personal-workspace placement; unchanged draft/caret; no action merely from selecting/moving/hiding; explicit original submission and result; denial and occurrence retirement. Include two occurrences of the same subject. This is the missing acceptance case, not another global-panel clipping test.
4. Finish shared material fidelity, then attention selection and remaining control presentation under their real owners. Preserve existing causal baselines and failures. Do not call these corrections a substitute for step2.
5. Only after the software interaction matches the requirement, prepare a short human test of that experience. Record understanding, shape/text readability and comfortable interaction. Do not mark GUI/VR product acceptance from readiness, screenshots or a guided click sequence alone.

No product edits, new app runs, hardware actions or package work were performed by the coordinator for this review. Existing writer changes and failures remain preserved.
